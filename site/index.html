      Running a validator?

      Your validator is on this page as soon as it is bonded. Bookmark it with your moniker in the address: ?q=my-node

      1. Watch the signing window

        A validator is jailed when it misses more than 5,000 of the last 10,000 blocks, about 8 hours offline in a row. Jailing burns 1 % of the stake and lasts at least 10 min.

      2. Never run the key twice

        Two machines signing with the same key is a double signature: 5 % of the stake is burned and the validator is tombstoned for good. Keep a standby cold until the original is stopped.

      3. Unjail

        Once the jail time is over and the node has caught up again:

        latandad tx slashing unjail --from wallet --chain-id latanda-testnet-1 \
  --gas auto --gas-adjustment 1.5 --fees 5000ultd
